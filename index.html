A NOTE BEFORE YOU GO

More places. Honest details.

We search OpenStreetMap for toilets and facilities with mapped public restrooms, including parks. Private and customer-only facilities are excluded. Missing access details are labeled, not guessed.

Community data isn’t complete. Hours and access can change, and an “open now” status isn’t verified. A listed distance is to the mapped point, which may not be the entrance. “Near” a named park or landmark is nearby context, not a verified restroom name or entrance.

Download results for import into Google My Maps or other KML-compatible apps. This does not add places to Apple Maps or Google’s public listings.
